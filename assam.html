About Assam

Assam is a beautiful state located in North-East India. It is famous for its tea gardens, the Brahmaputra River, Kaziranga National Park and the one-horned rhinoceros. The capital of Assam is Dispur.

Famous Places

  • Kaziranga National Park
  • Majuli Island
  • Kamakhya Temple
  • Tea Gardens
